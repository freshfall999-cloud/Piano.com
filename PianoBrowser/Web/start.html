Connecting

  1. USB: plug the instrument in with a USB-C cable or a Lightning “Camera Adapter”. It appears above by itself.
  2. Bluetooth: tap the piano button in the toolbar, then “Bluetooth MIDI”.
  3. On your piano app, tap ••• in the address bar → “Set as Home Page” to open it in every new tab.
